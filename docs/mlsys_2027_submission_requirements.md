# MLSys 2027 submission requirements

Verified 2026-09-08 against the official
[Call for Research Papers](https://mlsys.org/Conferences/2027/CallForResearchPapers).
Recheck before actual submission; this note is not permission to submit.

- Submission deadline: October 30, 2026, 20:00 UTC (October31,05:00 KST).
- Main paper: at most10 pages, two columns, excluding references. Use the
  supplied `mlsys2025style.zip`; the2027 call explicitly retains that format.
- References must list all authors. Do not truncate bibliography author lists.
- Appendix: separate upload, same deadline; reviewers need not read it.
- Research track: double-blind, including affiliations and implicit identity
  disclosure. Artifact/source links must follow anonymization requirements.
- Non-archival workshop overlap is an exception to dual-submission rules.
  Expanded archival workshop papers require program-chair approval and an
  explanation of the added novelty. The submitted AXIOM paper's archival status
  is not yet verified here. Preserve it unchanged and prepare a differences note;
  do not contact chairs or submit without the user's explicit authorization.
- The call permits consulting LLMs for writing but prohibits papers generated
  by generative models alone (apart from specified illustrative/experimental
  content). The user must substantively review, understand and take authorship
  responsibility for the research and paper. Agent-produced text is a draft,
  not automatically a policy-compliant submission.

Current work remains experiments plus a separate MLSys draft. Publication,
chair correspondence, authorship decisions and submission are not authorized.
Do not label the paper submission-ready while relevant eligibility, author
review, scientific-evidence or formatting requirements remain unresolved.
